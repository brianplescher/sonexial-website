# Press Kit & Media Asset Kit - Setup Guide

## 1. Google Form Setup
Create a new Google Form with these questions:

### Section 1: The Hook
*   **Book Title & Subtitle** (Short Answer)
*   **The "One-Sentence" Pitch** (Paragraph): "In 25 words or less, what is the core promise of your book?"
*   **Target Audience** (Short Answer): "Who is this book specifically for?"
*   **Top 3 Comparative Titles** (Paragraph): "List books or authors that share your audience."

### Section 2: Author Authority & Assets
*   **Key Credentials** (Paragraph): "What degrees, career history, or life experiences make you the authority on this topic?"
*   **The "Unexpected" Fact** (Short Answer): "One surprising/quirky fact for icebreaker questions."
*   **Media Assets** (Short Answer): "Link to Google Drive/Dropbox folder with headshot and cover."

### Section 3: Core Message
*   **The "Aha" Moment** (Paragraph): "What is the single most important takeaway?"
*   **Primary Links** (Paragraph): "Website and social media links."
