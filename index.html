THE PROBLEM

The old playbook is broken.

For fifteen years, authors were told to upload to Amazon, optimize keywords, run ads, and pray. In 2026, that advice is a slow-motion burial.

01

Amazon is a checkout counter

It can sell books, but it does not discover them for you. Readers are increasingly asking AI tools for recommendations before they ever click into your KDP listing.

02

Google sends fewer clicks

AI Overviews answer more questions directly, which means your website has to do the work of making you discoverable, credible, and quotable.

03

Most author sites are invisible to AI

Even polished sites often lack the semantic structure, entity clarity, schema markup, and AI-readable signals that modern discovery systems actually use.

THE SHIFT

Meet GEO: the SEO of the AI era.

Search Engine Optimization made you findable on Google. Generative Engine Optimization makes your author platform easy for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and AI search interfaces to understand, cite, and recommend to readers.

01
Clean semantic site structure

AI answer engines can easily parse, quote, and recommend sites that organize your books, themes, and reading orders clearly.

02
Clear author & book knowledge structure

Eliminates confusion between your name, pen names, titles, genres, and platforms so AI systems never mix up your work or hallucinate details.

03
Machine-readable schema & llms.txt

Hidden structured code and an AI roadmap file that tell crawlers exactly who you are, what books you write, and where official retail links live.
