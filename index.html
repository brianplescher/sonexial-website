THE PROBLEM

The old playbook is broken.

For fifteen years, authors were told to upload to Amazon, optimize keywords, run ads, and pray. In 2026, that advice is a slow-motion burial.

01

Amazon is a checkout counter

It can sell books, but it does not discover them for you. Readers are increasingly asking AI tools for recommendations before they ever click into your KDP listing.

02

Google sends fewer clicks

AI Overviews answer more questions directly, which means your website has to do the work of making you discoverable, credible, and quotable.

03

Most author sites are invisible to AI

Even polished sites often lack the semantic structure, entity clarity, schema markup, and AI-readable signals that modern discovery systems actually use.

THE SHIFT

Meet GEO: the SEO of the AI era.

Search Engine Optimization made you findable on Google. Generative Engine Optimization helps make your author platform easier for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and AI search interfaces to understand, summarize, and surface.

01
Clean semantic structure

LLMs can parse and quote sites that are clearly organized around topics, entities, and reader intent.

02
Verifiable entity data

Who you are, what you write, what you publish, and where readers can find you — all structured for machines.

03
Schema, Q&A content, and llms.txt

These signals help AI systems understand your site, connect your author entity to your books, and evaluate your content for possible citation.
