THE PROBLEM

The old playbook is broken.

For fifteen years, businesses and authors were told to launch a pretty brochure site, stuff a few keywords, run ads, and wait. In 2026, that advice is a slow-motion burial.

01

Directories & marketplaces are checkout counters

Google Business profiles, directories, and Amazon can close someone who already knows your name—but buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews who to hire or read before they ever click a listing.

02

Google sends fewer clicks

AI Overviews answer high-intent comparisons directly, which means your website has to do the heavy lifting of making your services, credentials, and catalog verifiable and quotable.

03

Most business & author sites are invisible to AI

Even expensive, polished websites routinely lack the semantic heading hierarchy, entity clarity, JSON-LD schema markup, and crawler rules that modern retrieval systems use.

THE SHIFT

Meet GEO: the SEO of the AI era.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) builds on proven technical SEO with accessible pages, factual service or catalog content, and explicit structured data. These foundations help search tools interpret your business accurately, though no ethical service can guarantee placement in an AI-generated answer.

01
Clean semantic site structure

Clear H1/H2 headings, explicit service scopes, and structured FAQs help human buyers and automated parsers extract key facts without guesswork.

02
Connected entity & credential graph

Clarifies relationships between your organization, founders, practice areas, books, locations, and verified external profiles, reducing entity ambiguity.

03
Machine-readable JSON-LD & crawler clarity

Structured data describes your services, FAQs, and official profiles, paired with clean robots.txt search-bot directives and an optional llms.txt overview.
