FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Understanding Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

How does accurate JSON-LD schema help my business or author site?

Accurate Schema.org structured data (such as Organization, LocalBusiness, ProfessionalService, Service, FAQPage, or Person and Book) helps AI search systems and crawlers interpret your identity, services, credentials, and FAQs without guesswork.

What is llms.txt, and is it required?

An /llms.txt file is an optional proposed text overview for compatible retrieval tools. Major AI search products have not established it as a requirement for citation or discovery—clear on-page content, JSON-LD schema, and crawl access matter most.

What if my site is blocking AI crawlers in robots.txt?

Check access for search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and Googlebot. Training crawler policies (for example GPTBot or ClaudeBot) are separate choices and are not scored as search visibility failures.
