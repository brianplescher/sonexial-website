Why Sonexial exists

For fifteen years, the self-publishing playbook was Amazon-first: upload, optimize, advertise, repeat. That worked when there were 200,000 KDP titles. There are now over two million. The math has broken.

At the same time, readers are increasingly discovering books through Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini — and those systems cite websites, not KDP pages.

Sonexial exists to help authors adapt to that reality with built assets: websites, kits, and infrastructure that keep working while you write the next book.
